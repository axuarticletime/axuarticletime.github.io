AdSense Approval Tips

By Ubaid Ali — 2025-08-11
Tags: AdSense, Blogging, Monetization

So, all you thought of was getting some extra bucks, but you ended up discouraged because of AdSense emails.

Many bloggers face the same challenge. The reasons for rejection are often simple, but easy to overlook. Let’s go through some common factors that can affect approval.

1. Domain and Hosting Requirements

Google AdSense requires a solid domain and reliable hosting with some age to build trust. Applying immediately after buying a domain rarely works. Patience is essential. Approval time can vary from a few months to longer depending on your content.

Blogger sites can be approved if they provide quality, original content. Free WordPress.com blogs don’t allow AdSense, while self-hosted WordPress.org does. Using your own custom domain with hosting usually gives you more control and higher chances of approval.

2. Importance of Traffic for AdSense

Even if you have strong content, without visitors, it won’t matter. AdSense doesn’t specify a required number of visits, but consistent organic and social traffic is necessary to make earnings possible. Sharing your site on platforms like Facebook, Pinterest, Quora, and Twitter can help generate visibility.

3. Patience During the Review Process

You may receive rejections from AdSense at first. Don’t get discouraged — identify the problems, fix them, and reapply. Building a site that meets policy standards takes time.

4. Common Drawbacks of Free Websites

Many people want to start websites without spending money. While some free options exist, results are not always guaranteed. A custom domain and hosting provide more flexibility and professionalism. Remember — steady effort and patience pay off.

5. Simple Advice for New Bloggers

Even if your website is approved, think beyond approval. Running a site has costs — domain, hosting, maintenance — and revenue depends on real visitors, not shortcuts. Many users also run ad blockers, which can lower earnings.

Ad revenue varies widely. Some ads may pay very little per impression, while others may pay several dollars per thousand impressions depending on niche and audience location. Growth comes from consistent traffic and quality content, not quick tricks.

Using bots or fake traffic is a direct violation of AdSense policies and can lead to a permanent ban. Always focus on genuine visitors.

A final tip: Don’t waste money on courses or services that promise instant earnings. They often rely on unsafe methods that Google will detect. Be careful, stay safe, and build your site step by step.

Key Takeaways

  • Use a custom domain with reliable hosting to build trust with AdSense.
  • Focus on generating consistent, real traffic — social media can help.
  • Be patient; approvals may take time.
  • Avoid bots or shortcuts — they risk account termination.
  • Plan for long-term costs and set realistic expectations about revenue.
