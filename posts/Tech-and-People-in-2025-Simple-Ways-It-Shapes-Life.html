The Good and the Bad

Tech isn’t perfect. It can raise problems like:

  • Privacy: Apps might track what you do if you’re not careful.
  • Screen Time: Too much tech can make you feel hooked or stressed.
  • Fake News: Wrong info spreads fast online.
  • Environment: Making devices can harm the planet if not done right.

To stay safe, use strong passwords, limit screen time, check facts, and choose eco-friendly brands. Tech should help, not control you.
